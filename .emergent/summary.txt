**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
- **Roles Hierarchy**: Admin -> Maalik (owners) -> Muneem (Senior field agent) -> Sipahi (Junior field agent).
- **Geography**: Maaliks own Illakas (areas). Muneems are assigned to Illakas. Sipahis are assigned to Illakas and can access all Misals (villages) within them.
